# Audio sources and licensing

## Hindi speech

The application currently uses browser Hindi speech synthesis as its fallback. The preferred future source for exact recorded Hindi stimuli is Mozilla Common Voice Hindi.

- Mozilla Common Voice Hindi — CC0 1.0
- Common Voice Scripted Speech 27.0 - Hindi
- Locale: hi
- Format: MP3
- Dataset size listed by Mozilla: 546.57 MB
- Source: https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/ia/datasets

Only exact matching recordings should be substituted into the clinical stimulus bank. Unrelated corpus clips should not be concatenated to manufacture a sentence.

## Clinical status

Audio selection and loudness have not been clinically calibrated. Environmental recordings are for auditory-training practice, not validated acoustic assessment. Speech-in-noise remains uncalibrated until controlled SNR tracks are added.
